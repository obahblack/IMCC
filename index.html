About IMCC

A Conference Built for
Missionary Children

IMCC — the International Missionary Kids Conference — is a dedicated gathering created for children and families connected to missionary work around the world.

Growing up as a missionary child means navigating multiple cultures, languages, schools, and ministry environments. IMCC provides a unique space where these children can connect with others who truly understand their experiences — and be reminded that they belong to a global family of faith.

Through worship, teaching, mentorship, creative activities, and cross-cultural friendship, IMCC helps the next generation grow stronger in identity, purpose, and community.
